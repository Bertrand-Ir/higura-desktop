# Higura for desktop

Higura as an app on your computer: its own window, tabs, and it opens straight to your business.

| | Download |
|---|---|
| **Windows** 10 / 11 | [Higura-Setup.exe](https://github.com/Bertrand-Ir/higura-desktop/releases/latest/download/Higura-Setup.exe) |
| **Mac** (Apple Silicon & Intel) | [Higura.pkg](https://github.com/Bertrand-Ir/higura-desktop/releases/latest/download/Higura.pkg) |

You can also download it from Higura: profile menu → **Download Higura**.

## Installing

**Windows:** open `Higura-Setup.exe`. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Higura installs and opens by itself.

**Mac:** open `Higura.pkg`. If your Mac says it can't be opened, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**, then follow the installer. Higura opens when it finishes.

The warning appears because the installer isn't code-signed yet. It is safe to continue.
